# LinkedIn Outreach Kit — Originator Recruiting (The West)

## The honest word on "automated and executable"

You asked for automated LinkedIn outreach. Here is the real trade-off, and my recommendation:

**LinkedIn aggressively bans accounts that use sending bots** (Expandi, Dripify, LinkedHelper,
etc. all operate against LinkedIn's User Agreement). For most people that's an annoyance. For a
team leader running a recruiting campaign under his own name at a Rithm-owned lender, losing your
LinkedIn — your network, your credibility, mid-campaign — is a campaign-ending event. Response
rates on botted messages are also 3-5x worse than personalized ones in a small, gossipy industry
where everyone knows everyone. RTL is a ~200-person world west of Texas at the senior level. One
"this feels like a bot" screenshot in a group chat and the well is poisoned.

**What I recommend instead — "automated everything except the click":**

1. The roster (in `/growth/roster/`) gives you the named targets and where to find them.
2. This kit gives you a personalized message for every situation — copy, paste, send.
3. The tracker (`/growth/trackers/recruiting-pipeline-tracker.csv`) tells you each day who to
   touch and what to say next.
4. I (Claude) can run a recurring session that: reads the tracker, generates that day's batch of
   15 personalized messages with names/companies filled in, drafts follow-up emails in your Gmail
   for anyone who's shared an email, and updates the tracker from your notes. Your job shrinks to
   ~20 minutes/day of pasting and sending — which also keeps you inside LinkedIn's limits
   (~100 connection requests/week, and you want to stay under ~15-20/day).
5. **LinkedIn Recruiter Lite or Sales Navigator** ($170/mo) is the one tool worth buying: 30
   InMails/month to people outside your network, unlimited search by title + geography + company.

If after reading this you still want a third-party sending tool, use a separate "recruiting"
profile, not your main one — but I'd start manual-assisted for 30 days and look at the reply data.

## Daily cadence (20 min, before the market opens)

| Step | Time | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 min | Open tracker, sort by "Next Action Date" = today |
| 2 | 10 min | Send 10-15 connection requests / InMails using templates below |
| 3 | 5 min | Log sends, replies, and stage changes in tracker |

Weekly target: 50-75 new touches, 15-20 replies, 5-8 calls booked. At those rates the funnel
math in PLAN.md produces 2-3 hires per quarter.

## Pipeline stages (use these exact values in the tracker)

`1-Identified → 2-Outreach sent → 3-Replied → 4-Call done → 5-Interview loop → 6-Offer → 7-Hired`
plus `8-Not now (nurture)` and `9-Dead`. Anyone in "8" gets one touch per quarter forever —
originators change jobs on a 2-4 year clock and the "not now" pile is where 2027's hires live.

## Templates

Rules: under 300 characters for connection notes (LinkedIn limit). Never mention their current
employer's problems in writing. Never put comp in writing before a call. First-person, no
recruiter-speak ("opportunity," "reach out," "touch base" are banned words).

### A. Cold connection request (the workhorse)
> Hi {First} — I run an originations team at Genesis Capital. I keep hearing your name come up
> in the {metro} fix-and-flip world. No pitch — just like knowing the strong producers out West.
> Open to connecting?

### B. Cold connection request, variant for award/ranking winners
> {First} — saw you on the {Scotsman Guide / AAPL} list. Congrats, that volume in this market is
> real work. I run originations at Genesis Capital and try to know everyone doing it at that
> level out West. Would be glad to connect.

### C. After they accept (within 24h, this is where campaigns die — don't skip)
> Appreciate the connect, {First}. I'll be direct because you're busy: I'm growing my team at
> Genesis and {metro} is a market where I want more coverage. I'm not assuming you're looking —
> the strong ones never are — but I'd value 15 minutes to compare notes on the market either way.
> Coffee or a call next week?

### D. InMail (when not connected; you get ~30/mo with Recruiter Lite)
> Subject: {Metro} + Genesis Capital
>
> {First} — I lead an originations team at Genesis Capital ($75M+/mo and scaling). I'm adding
> experienced RTL originators in the West and your name is one of the few that came up more than
> once. Two honest facts: balance-sheet certainty here means your deals close, and my best people
> out-earn their last shop because nothing falls out at the table. Worth 15 minutes? If not, no
> follow-up — I respect a clean no.

### E. Follow-up #1 (5-7 days after C/D, no reply)
> {First} — one nudge and then I'll leave you alone. Even if Genesis is never interesting, I'm a
> good person for you to know: I see most of the West's RTL flow. 15 minutes, zero pitch unless
> you ask.

### F. Follow-up #2 / breakup (10-14 days later — highest reply rate of the sequence)
> No reply needed, {First} — I'll assume the timing's wrong and check back in a few months.
> If anything changes at {Company} before then, my line's open. Good selling.

### G. Warm intro ask (use on mutual connections — converts 5-10x better than cold)
> {Mutual first name} — quick favor. Do you know {Target} at {Company} well enough to intro me?
> Growing my team at Genesis and I'd rather come in warm. Happy to return the favor; I owe you
> one either way.

### H. The "disruption" message (for originators at shops with layoffs / wind-downs / capital problems — see roster "Recruiting angle" column)
> {First} — I won't pretend I don't read the trades. Whatever's true about {Company}, your
> production speaks for itself, and people who can originate through THIS market are exactly who
> I'm hiring at Genesis. If it's useful to talk — confidentially, no process, just straight
> answers about what a book like yours earns here — I'm around this week.

### I. Conference pre-book (2-3 weeks before AAPL / NPLA / IMN — see roster for 2026 dates)
> {First} — looks like we'll both be at {conference}. I'm setting up coffees with the producers
> I rate out West. 20 minutes, on me, no agenda you don't set. What day works?

## The 30-second verbal pitch (for when they say "okay, tell me about Genesis")

Have this cold. Tune it once with real numbers, then everyone on the interview loop uses the
same one: (1) **Certainty** — balance-sheet lender backed by Rithm; no fund-redemption pauses, no
relisting your borrower's deal to another capital partner; what we term-sheet, we close.
(2) **Product width** — bridge, fix-and-flip, ground-up, and scale construction — you never lose
a borrower because we lack the box. (3) **Your economics** — bps on funded volume with no house
account games, and ops support that lets a $8M/mo producer do $12M here. Then stop talking and
ask about THEIR book.

## Legal guardrails (2 minutes, saves a lawsuit)

- Ask every serious candidate for their **non-solicit / non-compete** terms before the offer
  stage; log it in the tracker column. California voids most non-competes (and since 2024
  employers must not even try to enforce them) — but **non-solicits of customers can still bite,
  and candidates from non-CA states (AZ, NV, UT, ID, CO has limits) may have enforceable paper.**
- Never ask candidates to bring borrower lists, pipeline reports, or any document from their
  current employer. Their relationships come in their head or not at all.
- Run offer-stage candidates past Genesis HR/legal — you're at a Rithm company; use that air cover.

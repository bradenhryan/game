## Coverage gaps
NM / MT / WY / AK have no sizable homegrown RTL lenders — they're served by Aloha Capital,
Juniper Capital, Payette, and the nationals. Recruit for those territories from the nationals'
remote AE benches (RCN, LendingOne, Temple View, Constructive).
